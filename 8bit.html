STAGE 2 PHYSICS · MOMENTUM

The scooter lesson

She throws the ball forward and slides backward on the board, and the whole class steps in. Conservation of momentum stopped being a formula and became something we all felt.

? KNOWLEDGE QUESTION

When a law is felt in the body, do we know it differently?

STAGE 3 THEN & LATER

Two moments, one student

The tears on the left and the 100% on the right belong to the same girl. The line between them is the resilience we built together, and I’m proud of both.

? KNOWLEDGE QUESTION

How does failure become knowledge?

STAGE 4 PHYSICS · CS · LIVE DATA

Running the data

We took the lesson outside. The drone recorded all of us running, and that recording was the point: tracker analysis of the footage, combined with the drone’s sensor telemetry, turned our own motion into a motion graph.

? KNOWLEDGE QUESTION

What changes when the data is our own movement?

STAGE 5 AFTER SCHOOL

Who is stopping you from asking questions and learning?

She came after school, lost, with an assessment the next day and big targets. I told her she could go home, or we could stay together until the concepts made sense. I’m here, and it’s your choice. She stayed, asked question after question, and left a different person.

? KNOWLEDGE QUESTION

Is resilience something we choose?

Where the waves overlap, learning happens.
