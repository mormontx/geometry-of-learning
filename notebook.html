IVPhysicsCS · live data

Running the data

We took the lesson outside. The drone recorded all of us running, and that recording was the point: tracker analysis of the footage, combined with the drone’s sensor telemetry, turned our own motion into a motion graph.

Knowledge question

What changes when the data is our own movement?

Who is stopping you from asking questions and learning?

She came after school, lost, with an assessment the next day and big targets. I told her she could go home, or we could stay together until the concepts made sense. I’m here, and it’s your choice. She stayed, asked question after question, and left a different person.
